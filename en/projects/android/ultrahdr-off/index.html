Case study · Android app

UltraHDR Off

Converts Ultra HDR photos into standard SDR copies directly on the Android device. It covers the case where galleries, apps and devices that are not fully Ultra HDR compatible display those photos differently: the app selects the Ultra HDR images and produces copies that are easier to view, share or manage, without touching the originals.

Application published on Google Play by Rios Studio. It has no public repository or open source code: the Google Play listing is the only official source for this page.

From the problem to the output

Photos that other readers do not display well

  1. 01

    Problem

    An Ultra HDR photo carries high-brightness information in addition to the standard dynamic range. Galleries, apps and devices that do not interpret that format display it differently, and workflows that only accept standard SDR do not process it clearly.

  2. 02

    Solution

    An app focused on a single task: select one or more photos, detect which ones are Ultra HDR and generate independent SDR copies, saved in a separate folder and keeping the relevant metadata.

  3. 03

    Result

    SDR copies ready to view, share or manage in apps without Ultra HDR support, with the original photos untouched and all processing executed on the device.

Flow

How the conversion works

General flow stated in the Google Play listing and visible in its screenshots. Implementation documentation is not public.

  1. 01

    Selection

    One or more photos are chosen. The picker can filter by folder (All, the copies folder Pictures/SDR-Copies and a Demo folder), and multiple selection works with long press and dragging, with a counter of photos pending.

  2. 02

    Detection

    Before converting, the app shows each photo’s name, resolution, size and whether it contains Ultra HDR.

  3. 03

    Conversion

    It generates the standard SDR copy named <original>_SDR.jpg in the output folder and keeps the relevant metadata; if that name already exists, the configured duplicate rule applies.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    When it finishes it reports the batch result: successful copies, failed copies and the folder where they were saved. The originals stay untouched.
