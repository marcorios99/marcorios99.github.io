Local AI

Models running on owned hardware, with their deployments, their tests and their guides. Each entry is a standalone page that can be read on its own: MoE inference on a 24 GB GPU, a benchmark against a live server, and how to start the engine.

What is mine here is the deployment, the harness and the measurements. The engine is a fork of Strata maintained by MistVVK, the weights are Qwen's compressed by ISTA-DASLab and agentionai, and the loop guard comes from Project Maya. This folder will keep adding entries; the next ones planned are a catalog of local weights and image and video generation with ComfyUI, not published yet.

The machine

The hardware everything runs on

A desktop PC with an Intel GPU, 32 GB of RAM and NVMe. The specifications were read from the hardware (lspci, /proc/cpuinfo, dpkg and the engine's /metrics), not from the product page. The weights live on an NVMe drive separate from the system one.

GPU and CPU
Intel Arc Pro B60 24 GB · Intel i5-12400F · 32 GB of RAM.
System
Ubuntu 24.04.5 LTS · kernel 7.0.0-43-generic · NVMe WD Blue SN570.
Engine and model
XeStrata xe0.2.8-1 running qwen3.8-flash-next-gyro-gyro-s (TQ1_0), 131,072-token context.

Data

What is published, and how to read it

The numbers in the three pages come from a single run. The published file is summarized; the full raw output will live in the harness repository.

Published today
1 run · 49 cases · 47 matched requests · 3 pages · 1 harness.
File
run-2026-11-14.json, schema gyro-bench-run/2: 49 cases with their metrics, the thinking levels, concurrency and stability.
Also includes
The run's options (--seed 7 --repeat 3), the counters of matched requests, and the /health and /metrics snapshots.
Comparing runs
Another run with the same options is comparable; without a seed, reasoning changes between runs.

Scope

What is measured and what is still missing

Measured on this PC

Still outside
