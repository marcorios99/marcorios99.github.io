Local AI

Models running on owned hardware, with their deployments, their tests and their guides. Each entry has its own page: the XeStrata deployment, the gyro-bench harness, and how to start the engine.

What is mine here is the deployment, the harness and the measurements. The engine is a fork of Strata maintained by MistVVK, the weights are Qwen's compressed by ISTA-DASLab and agentionai, and the loop guard comes from Project Maya. This folder will keep adding entries; the next one planned is image generation with ComfyUI, not published yet.

Data

What is published, and how to read it

The numbers in the three pages come from a single run. The published file is summarized; the full raw output will live in the harness repository.

File
run-2026-11-14.json, schema gyro-bench-run/2: 49 cases with their metrics, the thinking levels, concurrency and stability.
Also includes
The run's options (--seed 7 --repeat 3), the counters of matched requests, and the /health and /metrics snapshots.
Comparing runs
Another run with the same options is comparable; without a seed, reasoning changes between runs.

Scope

What is measured and what is still missing

Measured on this PC

  • One quantization (Gyro-S TQ1_0) and one card (Intel Arc Pro B60).
  • One installation route (from the source) and one run with a fixed seed.
  • Specifications read from lspci, /proc/cpuinfo, dpkg and the engine's /metrics.

Still outside

  • Sustained or multi-user performance: the engine serves one request at a time.
  • The other quantizations, AMD or NVIDIA GPUs, and the deb/rpm packages.
  • Image generation with ComfyUI: the next entry planned for this folder.
